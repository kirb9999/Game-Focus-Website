INTRO

Jet Set Radio is a 2000 action-platform video game developed by Smilebit and published by Sega for the Dreamcast.You, as the player, controls a member of the GG's, a youth gang that uses inline skates to traverse Tokyo while spraying graffiti, challenging rival gangs, and evading authorities.
