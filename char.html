Beat is a 17 year old Rudie living in Tokyo-to, after running away from home in Fukuoka. He is a member and founder of the GG's along with Gum and Corn, and is the leader of the gang as well. He is one of the three playable characters the player starts with in Jet Set Radio, and is considered to be the Jet Set Radio series mascot.

Beat is a laid back and level headed Rudie who ran away from home and helped found the GG's. Beat loves to skate and is okay with breaking the law and is originally from the city of Fukuoka. Beat spends his time skating around fighting for turf with the rival gangs of Tokyo-to, marking his territory with graffiti and annihilating others. tags

Beat is a Technique type character, but has very in the middle stats. His biggest saving grace being his health bar. He is a beginner-friendly character to use for first time players.

Gum is a 17 year old Rudie living in Tokyo-to. She is the one of the main playable characters and could be considered as the Jet Set Radio series secondary mascot. She, alongside Corn and Beat, is an original member of the GG's and a loyal friend to her allies.

Gum is a Graffiti type character, meaning that she can score more points while tagging compared to most characters. However, her biggest downsides are her 15 spray can limit and her health is on the lower side than usual.

Gum is described as a perverse character who likes chewing gum. Her high Graffiti stat implies a natural affinity for art. Her willingness to show Beat the basics about the streets implies she is quite friendly.

Tab, also known as Corn in other versions of the game, is a 16 year old Rudie living in Tokyo-to as a member of the GG's. He is a playable character in the Jet Set Radio series. He is one of the first three characters the player can choose after the opening tutorials of Jet Set Radio, along with Beat and Gum.

Tab is a Technique type character, but unlike Beat, his biggest compliment is his spray can capacity of 30 cans. However, due to this advantage, his other stats are just somewhat okay, as his biggest drawback is the lack of points he gets compared to the other characters while tagging

Tab is consistently described as a genius,he's presumably somewhat arrogant with general enthusiasm for his work. The dialogue he has during his challenges seems to indicate this arrogance.

Yo-Yo is a 16 year old Rudie living in Tokyo-to. He is a playable character and member of the GG's in Jet Set Radio. He joins the GG's after the player beats him in a race around Benten-cho.

Yo-Yo is a Graffiti type character who excels in graffiti, but also speed, or technique, as he is one of the more faster characters of the cast. Similar to Gum,Yo-Yo's biggest drawbacks are his health and spray can limit of 15 cans,

Yo-Yo is described as a liar who likes to either use or sell counterfeit brands, implying him to be a deceptive person. He likes to say "yo" a lot when performing tricks and likes to do spins in the air, showing an energetic and bombastic side of him.

He calls the GG's a "bunch of slow pokes"

Mew, or Bis in Japan, is a 16 year old Rudie living in Tokyo-to. She is a supporting and playable character in Jet Set Radio. Mew joins the GG's after the player proves their skills by copying her moves via the challenges she provides them.

Mew is a Technique character whose best specialty is her speed, along with her graffiti stat. However, despite these strengths, her health is average, and her spray can limit caps off at 20 cans.

Mew is a joyful, independent, witty, and serious gangster who has an interest in money, wealth, and extravagance. She's a devil's advocate, being willing to entertain opinions she doesn't necessarily agree with. Her trademark graffiti implies she has an interest in music."Think you can follow me?"

Cube is a 20 year old Rudie from Grind City who came to Tokyo-to with Combo while trying to find their fellow gang member, Coin. She is a playable character and a member of the GG's in Jet Set Radio.

Cube is a Technique type character who slightly differs from the other types due to her unique spray can limit of 25 cans, which helps her when it comes to tagging. Her other stats are slightly lesser, but do not really hinder her in capacity.

Cube is a cool Rudie who likes skeletons, which is reflected in her graffiti pieces as all of her tags feature a skull, in addition to her wearing a skull belt too. Her trait of being cool is reflected by her fashion style according to her profile from the Perfect Guide, mentioning her lipstick, a tattoo and the aforementioned skull belt.

Combo is a 23 year old Rudie who originates from Grind City and later came to Tokyo-to with Cube in search for their fellow gang member, Coin. He is a playable character and a member of the GG's in Jet Set Radio.

Combo is a Technique type character who best excels in power, or health, as he is one of the few characters in the game to have a nearly full health bar unlike most of the cast. His biggest drawbacks however are his size and slow speed,

Combo is a violent Rudie who has a fondness for extra-large stuff,Despite the rough-looking appearance, his narration in the cutscenes suggests him to be well-spoken and introspective.
